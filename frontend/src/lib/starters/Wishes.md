# Wishes

Some notes deserve attention you have not given them yet. A wish is how you say so.

Open a note, find the wish action in its details, and once per day you may push that star a little brighter. It counts as a visit, the same ladder that [[Bright and Dim]] describes, advanced by one.

One wish per day across the whole sky. Spend it on the note you want to find again tomorrow, not the one you will open anyway.

Wishes are the only brightness that comes from intention instead of habit. A brilliant star fed entirely on wishes tells a true story too: something you keep deciding matters, even before it does.
