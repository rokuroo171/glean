# Starlines

A starline is glean's word for a link between two notes. You write it as double brackets around a note title, like this: `[[Wishes]]`. While editing, the brackets show as a dim pair around the caret; the note picker opens when you type them, or use Add starline in the editor menu.

Starlines do two jobs.

They are navigation. Click one and the target note opens, wherever it lives in the sky.

They are structure. Every starline between two notes draws a faint trail on the constellation, and visiting both ends of a trail strengthens it. The lines you use get brighter and more direct; the ones you never travel fade. Within a few days the constellation stops being a map of every note and becomes a map of how you actually think, drawn from the routes you take.
