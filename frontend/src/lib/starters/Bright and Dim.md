# Bright and Dim

Stars in your sky are not all the same brightness. That is the point.

Every note tracks how often you open it. One visit and it is a faint speck. Come back a few times and it dims in as a star. Keep returning and it climbs: steady, bright, and eventually a brilliant beacon you can spot across the whole constellation.

So a pale star means one of two things: a note you have not needed yet, or a note you are about to forget. glean shows you the difference by doing nothing at all. Brightness is just your own attention, reflected back.

Visits are counted when you open a note from the constellation, a search, or a [[Starlines|starline]]. Editing alone does not count; coming back does.
